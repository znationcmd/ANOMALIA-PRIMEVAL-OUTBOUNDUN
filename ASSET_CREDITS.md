# Graphical assets

Photographic surface scans by Poly Haven, distributed under CC0:
https://polyhaven.com/license

- Concrete Wall 001: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_001
- Aerial Grass Rock: https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock
- Asphalt 02: https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02
- Rocky Terrain 02: https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain_02
- Sand 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/sand_01

Diffuse textures are bundled as 768 px JPEG data URLs for fully offline use.
Facades, vegetation, architecture, sky and water shaders are created for this project.
The current scans use albedo-derived bump relief, not full scanned PBR normal maps.
No DayZ or ARK assets are included. This remains a pre-alpha prototype, not equivalent in production quality to either game.
